# Planning source research — 9 October 2026

This is a bounded source register for SitePivot screening, not an exhaustive NSW rules database or legal approval. Read-only research; no application code was changed by the researcher. Current primary sources were checked through both search engines, including the stronger official-source search. NSW legislation pages returned HTTP 403 on direct retrieval; indexed official legislation, Department guidance and council assessment material are distinguished below. Do not describe the entire legislation as fully retrieved or independently audited.

## Reviewed primary sources and usable inputs

### Housing SEPP: DA permission, development standards and subdivision differ

- [Current Housing SEPP](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/epi-2021-0714), Chapter 6; development standards s168; subdivision s169.
- [Department summary](https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/housing/low-and-mid-rise-housing-policy/summary-of-key-provisions), updated 21 March 2025, current retrieval 9 October 2026.
- Dual occupancy DA permission in R2 is distinct from the LMR standards. In a verified LMR area, dual occupancy standards apply in R1–R4 **where that use is permitted**: 450 m² site area, 12 m lot width, 0.65 FSR, 9.5 m height. Width must be measured at the front building line, not equated to road frontage. Subdivision is a separate R1/R2/R3 assessment: 225 m² and 6 m width per resulting lot. These are non-refusal standards for specified matters, not a guarantee of consent. Other less onerous applicable standards may remain relevant.

### Commencement and legal walking catchment

- [Department policy overview](https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/housing/low-and-mid-rise-housing-policy).
- Stage 1 commenced 1 July 2024; Stage 2 commenced 28 February 2025. Stage 2 concerns nominated centres/stations in Greater Sydney, Central Coast, Lower Hunter/Newcastle and Illawarra-Shoalhaven. LMR requires 800 m **walking** distance from the mapped town-centre edge or nominated station entrance, not an 800 m circular buffer or membership of an LGA/suburb.
- [Industry guidance](https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/housing/low-and-mid-rise-housing-policy/guidance-for-industry-developers-and-councils) and [FAQ](https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/housing/low-and-mid-rise-housing-policy/frequently-asked-questions) say indicative maps do not establish property applicability; constraints and walking paths require checking. All other applicable planning matters still need merit assessment.

### LMR exclusions — incomplete map results must remain unknown

- [Department exclusions page](https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/housing/low-and-mid-rise-housing-policy/low-and-mid-rise-housing-policy-exclusions), updated 24 April 2026.
- Exclusions include bushfire vegetation categories 1–3 **and buffers**; ANEF ≥25 / ANEC ≥20; specified Hawkesbury-Nepean and Georges River probable-maximum-flood land; flood-planning areas in the listed 22 LGAs; coastal wetlands/littoral rainforest/coastal vulnerability land; within 200 m of relevant dangerous-goods pipelines; TOD areas; heritage items; Bathurst, Blue Mountains, Hawkesbury and Wollondilly LGAs. Heritage conservation area membership alone is not the same as containing a heritage item.
- This page is guidance; evaluate legal scope, maps and all applicable exclusions. A clear flood layer or a negative exclusion-map query does not establish that all exclusions were checked. Existing local permissions can remain relevant on land excluded from the state policy.

### Northern Beaches: identify the actual LEP

- [Council current reform page](https://www.northernbeaches.nsw.gov.au/planning-and-development/planning-future/planning-growth/housing/low-and-mid-rise-housing-policy) confirms Warringah controls changed 31 October 2025 and DCP LMR amendments commenced 15 September 2025.
- [Warringah LEP current](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/epi-2011-0649), cl4.1B/4.1C. Indexed official history: cl4.1B and cl4.1C inserted by 2025 (597), Sch14[2]; cl1.8A savings also amended.
- [Council Local Planning Panel agenda, 4 February 2026](https://files.northernbeaches.nsw.gov.au/nbc-prod-files/media/files/2026-01/Agenda%20-%20Northern%20Beaches%20Local%20Planning%20Panel%20-%2020260204_0.pdf?1771154457=) expressly treats cl4.1B as requiring **at least 800 m²** in R2. A 634 m² proposal is assessed as a 20.75% variation under cl4.6 and recommended for refusal. Therefore use ≥800 as the ordinary benchmark; below it is a failed standard requiring lawful override/variation review, not categorical prohibition. Council web prose says “over 800”, but this assessment supplies the precise boundary.
- Savings, current exclusions, land-use table and detailed cl4.1C subdivision conditions require date-specific review. Do not infer subdivision from construction eligibility.
- [Council 2026 technical appendix](https://yoursay.northernbeaches.nsw.gov.au/download_file/view/12647/3398) confirms Pittwater cl4.1B 800 m² and Manly DCP cl4.1.1 1,000 m² in R2. This is official proposal evidence, not a replacement for the controlling instruments. Keep Manly/Pittwater rules review-only until their exact scope and current clauses are retrieved. Never label all Northern Beaches land Warringah.
- [Consolidated LEP project](https://yoursay.northernbeaches.nsw.gov.au/LEP-DCP) reports exhibition closed 30 September 2026. A draft is not an operative LEP; proposed 18 m frontage controls must not become current rules.

### Central Coast: attached and detached differ

- [Current Central Coast LEP 2022](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/epi-2022-0308), indexed current version from 1 July 2026. Official indexed cl4.1B supplies attached 550 m² / detached 700 m². Do not substitute the ordinary Lot Size Map subdivision value for these construction standards.
- Cl4.1C contains exceptions and must be separately resolved; the full operative text could not be retrieved in this research, so do not encode an unconditional subdivision permission/minimum from it.
- [Council LEP source](https://www.centralcoast.nsw.gov.au/plan-and-build/planning-controls/central-coast-local-environmental-plan-2022) and [2022 commencement notice](https://www.centralcoast.nsw.gov.au/council/media-release/reminder-central-coast-local-environmental-plan-and-central-coast-development) establish commencement 1 August 2022. Gosford City Centre and historically deferred land require their actual instrument; an LGA match alone is insufficient. Later deferred-land reforms may change coverage: use current parcel EPI evidence.

### CDC is a separate Codes SEPP regime

- [Current Codes SEPP](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/whole/html/inforce/current/epi-2008-0572), cl3B.8; indexed official clause found but full text blocked.
- [Department complying-development guide](https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-02/guide-to-complying-development.pdf), supporting guidance predating this review: width 15 m at building line, 12 m only with parking access solely from secondary/parallel road/lane. Parent lot is at least 400 m² and the applicable dual-occupancy minimum, whichever is greater. Housing Chapter6 DA non-refusal standards cannot replace the LEP CDC minimum. [Current Department code overview](https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/housing/low-rise-housing-diversity/low-rise-housing-diversity-code) requires all code/design standards and applicable permission. Keep CDC eligibility review-only without exact current code and full exclusion/design checks.

Sample 776 features were Ku-ring-gai precinct exclusions, illustrating why this layer alone cannot clear bushfire/heritage/flood/pipeline exclusions.

Preserve raw parcel-intersected attribute arrays, query URLs, retrieval time, source currency and failed queries. Use polygon intersection for site constraints rather than only a centroid. An empty response is evidence only for the layer queried, subject to complete response/pagination and current coverage; errors are unknown. Local LEP instrument names and clause4.1 mapping provenance must remain attached to ordinary subdivision thresholds.

## Independent review of planning-intelligence.js

Material issues reported to the implementing agent:

1. LMR standards do not establish R1/R3/R4 dual-occupancy permissibility; retain unknown without verified land-use permission.
2. Compute CDC from a separately resolved applicable local minimum. LMR winning the DA standard must not turn known Warringah800 or Central Coast550/700 CDC minimum into400; unknown applicable minimum must stay unknown.
3. The pre31October2025 application-date flag must not suppress unrelated Central Coast or Housing standards. Scope savings review to the relevant amendment; exact grandfather conditions are unresolved.
4. Require evidence for every relevant exclusion before an LMR override. Never clear all exclusions from layer776 alone.

These are screening rules with recorded limits, not an assertion that all NSW councils, all zones, every subdivision exception or every historic approval date is supported.
