# Premium development profitability repair — 12 October 2026

Baseline: `302084397960dc005aea9bdffb504561aebb9042`, sitepivot-concept. Founder-testing destination: https://boggedcake.github.io/ProjectIQ/. No main merge, hosting/visibility change or paid commitment.

## Defect and scope

Actual founder screenshots IMG_2898–2900 show 31 Seaview Street, Balgowlah, 672.4 m², R1, Manly Local Environmental Plan 2013, mapped FSR 0.5:1 and 8.5 m height. They show a conditional DA investigation, not a consent or CDC approval. The screenshots' $5.35m site value and $7m per dwelling are user working assumptions, not independent valuations. The old component amounts imply 400 m² above ground plus 200 m² basement (600 m² total); this is an inferred calibration scope, not surveyed plans. Statutory floor-area limits and construction gross area are separate; no compliant 600 m² design is asserted.

Profit was hidden by an unconditional UI requirement to enter outstanding land debt before invoking the shared feasibility engine. Unknown contributions subsequently withheld the result. The UI had only one midpoint feasibility and required callers to reach it through the detailed output. Broad low/high finish rates, soft-cost bands and unknown site allowances compounded uncertainty across every component. Four-level arrangements silently assumed two lifts, and there were no visible pool/lift allocation controls.

## Shared repair

The existing commercial engine, assessment and report remain the single model. A visible provisional-scenario control allows supported or conditional DA investigations to use disclosed editable assumptions. Unknown debt is modelled as zero solely for that sensitivity, never saved as confirmed debt-free. Contributions remain unverified: the UI offers an editable working amount, initially 1% of the delivered midpoint. This is a placeholder, not an assessment under a contribution plan. Strict mode with unknown debt or contributions withholds profit and returns an available subtotal. Invalid debt input is rejected in either mode. Failed standards, prohibition and unresolved unsupported pathways remain gated.

Main assessment, detailed feasibility and the printable/downloadable report use the same summary: GRV, economic development cost, profit, margin on revenue and return on cost. All three cost scenarios rerun finance and their full cost ledger. Site opportunity value is an economic cost, not a new cash purchase. Debt principal and refundable bonds are excluded from profit costs. Construction consultants, ordinary approvals, site works, contingency and construction GST are included once; excluded demolition, contributions, interest, holding, selling and provisional net tax adjustment are added once.

## Scope-specific construction

Total constructed area is split into above-ground and basement areas once. Specification selects the central hard rate; premium defaults to $4,500/m² above ground ex GST. ±8% above-ground uncertainty is a disclosed modelling assumption conditional on a fixed specification, not published statistical quantiles. Unknown basement/ground/access retain wider allowances. Confirmed ordinary scope uses narrower working bands; complex design, structure, restricted access, difficult ground and habitable basement finish add explicit scope. Rate overrides use the actual entered rates in calculations and reports. All-in builder/QS rates have inclusion controls for lifts/pools.

Lifts default to zero for every arrangement. Visible no/one/two/custom controls support up to ten, with per-home allocation and editable ex-GST unit allowance. Default ordinary allowance is $55k/$65k/$80k per lift, including ordinary installation/integration; exceptional shaft, structural or excavation scope requires separate confirmation.

Pools support none/shared/per-home/custom quantity, concrete/fibreglass, dimensions/depth, selected heating and editable component allowances. Displayed default 7 × 3 × 1.5 m dimensions and custom quantity one are persisted when selected. Ordinary excavation, shell/waterproofing, finishes, equipment, safety/fencing, heating, pool landscaping and design/approval are itemised. Pool design is excluded from the general consultant percentage; pool surrounds are excluded from general external works. Deselecting heating excludes its cost while retaining the editable amount. Exceptional rock, groundwater, retaining and access need additional pricing.
