# Contributions assessment — 16 October 2026

`contributions-evidence.js` is a pure UMD module (`ContributionsEvidence` / `SitePivotContributions`). `assess({property, project, assessmentDate, statutoryCostOfDevelopment, cost, provisionalAllowance})` returns an amount only with a supported framework/cost model or an explicitly accepted sourced allowance. Unknown amounts remain `null`, including separately listed HPC and utility/headworks charges. Framework evidence does not establish a Council payment quote.

## Verified framework sources

- [Northern Beaches current contributions register](https://www.northernbeaches.nsw.gov.au/planning-and-development/building-and-renovations/development-contributions) links the [adopted 2024 plan](https://www.northernbeaches.nsw.gov.au/media/65178). Effective 19 October 2024. Ordinary LGA scope excludes Dee Why Town Centre, Warriewood Valley Release Area and Frenchs Forest Town Centre. Plan §§2.4–2.8 prescribe nil through $100,000, 0.5% above $100,000 through $200,000, and 1% above $200,000, applied to the whole statutory cost. §§4.1–4.4 require cost-report validation, with an AIQS registered quantity surveyor for costs of $3 million or more. §§6.1–6.2 preserve earlier contribution conditions and original determinations for modifications. Manly LEP identity does not revive the repealed Manly s94 plan for a new application. §§2.5/6.5 require exemption/credit review; credit is not an automatic deduction for an existing dwelling. §6.3 requires payment-date indexation.
- [Kiama Council](https://www.kiama.nsw.gov.au/Plan-and-build/Plans-maps-and-controls/Development-contributions) and its [June 2024 plan](https://www.kiama.nsw.gov.au/files/sharedassets/public/v/2/plans/plans-of-management/24-68883-section-7.12-contribution-plan-2024-final-document-pdf.pdf) establish a whole-LGA s7.12 framework effective 28 June 2024 with the same ordinary bands. Determination date controls; modifications preserve the original plan. Council's live page continues to identify this plan despite the PDF's June 2026 review target. No automatic expiry is inferred from a review target.
- [Willoughby Council](https://www.willoughby.nsw.gov.au/Development/Plan/Development-Contributions) describes its mixed s7.11/s7.12 framework commencing 4 July 2019. The module identifies that framework and withholds a number pending development-class/catchment review; it does not generalise a 1% levy.
- [NSW Planning Portal guidance](https://www.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/local-infrastructure-contributions/making-7-12-contributions-plan) confirms ordinary prescribed maximum bands, special higher-rate areas, s208 cost requirements, the prohibition on s7.11 plus s7.12 on the same consent, and the prior-subdivision/additional-demand rule. Thus the ordinary bands are not a statewide universal levy.

The current legislation website could not be retrieved directly in this session. Section 208 wording was cross-checked in Kiama's official adopted plan §§10–11 and the NSW guidance. Statutory costs include works and associated demolition/site preparation/subdivision; exclusions cover land, finance/marketing, legal/project management, insurance, certain fittings, non-GST taxes/charges, accessibility and efficiency measures, affordable housing and heritage reuse. GST is retained. A DA-fee EDC or delivered feasibility total is not automatically the s208 base.

## Evidence and provisional model

For a base levy to be `assessed`, pass a verified catchment/source (Northern Beaches), development type, `consentPath` DA/CDC, `isNewApplication:true` or applicable original date, reviewed exemptions/credits/prior contributions and `statutoryCostOfDevelopment:{amount, verified:true, source, exclusionsReviewed:true, gstIncluded:true}`. This establishes base-date local levy arithmetic only; HPC, other authority charges and later indexation remain separate.

Working cost inputs return `provisional`, `decisionReady:false`, an editable allowance and missing evidence. The fallback component proxy includes selected building/site/consultant/contingency/demolition components plus 10% GST. It excludes the mixed approvals line and land/finance/selling. It must be replaced by the accepted s208 report: included consultants/contingency and excluded mixed fees can overstate or understate the statutory base. The proxy never promotes itself to verified evidence. No parcel address, total land valuation, dwelling-specific s7.11 tariff or indexed rate is hardcoded.

Dates beyond the source snapshot and saved determinations are blocked. Excluded catchments, claimed exemptions/credits, prior subdivision contributions without additional-demand verification and unknown councils require assessment. An explicitly accepted `provisionalAllowance:{amount, accepted:true, source}` can provide a visible working amount without pretending the statutory charge is resolved. A provisional zero never establishes a nil levy.
