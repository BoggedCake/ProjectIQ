# Duplex pathway evidence — reviewed 10 October 2026

This review separates land-use permission, parent-site standards, design assessment, subdivision and financial modelling. Independently sourced Manly LEP R1 permission supports **conditional preliminary costing**, subject to design and site checks. It does not establish an approved proposal, CDC eligibility, subdivision approval or decision-ready feasibility. Addresses are regression examples, never legal lookup keys or hardcoded exceptions.

## Ordinary Manly DA route

**Land Use Table, Zone R1, item3:** dual occupancies are permitted with development consent. Official indexed LEP2021-02-01 p15 expressly includes the use. The official2024-11-03 version, effective23 February2024–18 September2025, reproduces it (p16); a council-hosted Statement of Environmental Effects dated21 February2025 also reproduces R1 item3. **Clause4.1:** mapped minimum lot size applies to lots resulting from subdivision, with the clause's exceptions, including strata and community subdivision. ## CDC is a separate route

Codes SEPP Part3B Division2 applies to dual occupancies where no part of one dwelling is above another (cl3B.7). Stacked arrangements require the appropriate Division3 assessment; applying cl3B.8 indiscriminately to stacked duplexes is wrong.

**Clause3B.8(1):** parent lot area is at least the greater of400m² and the minimum lot area specified **for dual occupancies** in the applicable environmental planning instrument. An ordinary mapped subdivision minimum is not that use-specific minimum. **Clause3B.8(2):** width is measured at the building line:12m only where parent-lot parking is accessed solely from a secondary road, parallel road or lane; otherwise15m. Road frontage is not automatically building-line width.

The official2018 amendment establishes the area wording; the2020 amendment changes “lot” to “parent lot”; the2021 amendment establishes the parking-access condition. Current official Part3B indexing corroborates the parent-lot/greater400m² wording, but does not eliminate the current-text retrieval boundary above.

-2018 amendment: https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/EPIs/2018-132.pdf
-2020 amendment, Schedule1 items10–11: https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/pdf/asmade/epi-2020-331

Area and width are only initial CDC checks. Permissibility, cl1.17A/1.18/1.19, cl3B.2 exclusions, hazard requirements, design guide, height/GFA/setbacks/landscaping/parking and subdivision standards all remain. A mapped0.5 DA FSR is not automatically the Codes GFA calculation. A13m road frontage alone cannot establish either CDC refusal or eligibility without building-line geometry and parking access.

## Housing SEPP LMR route

Official Department summary, updated21 March2025 and retrieved10 October2026, states LMR areas are residential land within800m walking distance of nominated centres/station entrances, with hazard/constraint exclusions. Dual occupancy standards apply in R1–R4 **where permitted**:450m² lot,12m width,0.65:1 FSR,9.5m height and parking. These are non-refusal standards, not guaranteed consent. Unknown catchment/exclusions do not prove applicability and do not cancel separate LEP R1 permission.

- Department summary: https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/housing/low-and-mid-rise-housing-policy/summary-of-key-provisions
- Current Housing SEPP chapter6 (indexed;403 full retrieval): https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/pdf/inforce/current/epi-2021-0714%23ch.6.pdf

**Section168(2)** indexed official text measures12m at the front building line; parking minimum applies where no applicable instrument/DCP specifies a maximum. **Section169** indexed official2025-09-26 text applies to subdivision in verified LMR R1/R2/R3, with consent/application timing from28 February2025. Each resulting lot: no more than one dwelling,6m width at front building line, lawful access/frontage to public road,225m² area, no battle-axe; strata excluded. It must not be converted into an unconditional entitlement to subdivide.

## Product boundary

Permission can enable an explicit assumed design and preliminary cost range with unresolved checks visible. Decision-ready financial feasibility needs a validated envelope, construction area/scope, access/topography/sitework, services, market/value evidence and approval/title assumptions. Separate attached side-by-side, detached/front-rear and stacked arrangements; construction permission does not prove any chosen arrangement fits. Never claim approved, achievable, CDC eligible or subdividable from zoning and numeric benchmarks alone.
