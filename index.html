Northern Beaches planning context

Once the address is selected, HomeProjectIQ will identify the current planning instrument and applicable DCP family. For Class 1 residential alterations/additions DAs lodged from 1 January 2026, Council requires a Concept Stormwater Plan and engineer certification against its Water Management for Development Policy.
